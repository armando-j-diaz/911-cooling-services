### Decision order every round

```
R&D → Marketing → Production → HR (if shown) → Finance → Proforma check → Advance
```

### Cost-leader positioning rule

Set Performance **0.5 below** ideal and Size **0.3 above** ideal (bigger = worse = cheaper to make).

| Round | LT Perf | LT Size | HT Perf | HT Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4.8 | 15.0 | 7.6 | 12.2 |
| 2 | 5.3 | 14.5 | 8.3 | 11.5 |
| 3 | 5.8 | 14.0 | 9.0 | 10.8 |
| 4 | 6.3 | 13.5 | 9.7 | 10.1 |
| 5 | 6.8 | 13.0 | 10.4 | 9.4 |
| 6 | 7.3 | 12.5 | 11.1 | 8.7 |
| 7 | 7.8 | 12.0 | 11.8 | 8.0 |
| 8 | 8.3 | 11.5 | 12.5 | 7.3 |

### Forecast shortcut

After Round 1, always use Report → Marketing → your **Potential Sales × growth rate**:

- Low Tech: Potential Sales × **1.10**
- High Tech: Potential Sales × **1.20**

Round to nearest 50. **Never forecast higher than Beginning Inventory + Production After Adjustment.**

### Production schedule shortcut

```
Schedule ≈ (Forecast − Beginning Inventory) ÷ yield rate
```

| Round | Approx yield | Divide forecast by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 70% | 0.70 |
| 2 | 78% | 0.78 |
| 3+ | 85% | 0.85 |

# Troubleshooting (any round)

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Emergency loan in Proforma | Finance → **Aggressive** + issue bonds. Or cut ops spend to $0. |
| Stock-out (Report says "Yes") | Raise forecast 10%. Raise production schedule. Buy +200 capacity next round. |
| Excess inventory (>300 units) | Lower forecast to match actual sales. Lower schedule. Hurts Inventory Turnover star. |
| Contribution margin negative | Raise price $0.50. Or reduce production schedule. |
| Product outside perceptual map circle | Move perf/size toward the round's target in the table above. |
| Product 2 not selling | Check: was capacity bought in Round 1? Is revision date passed? Is forecast > 0? |
| Awareness below 30% | Increase promo to $1,500,000. Don't cut it later — 33% decays yearly. |
| Plant utilization > 150% | Buy +200 capacity. Overtime costs 50% more — capacity is cheaper. |
| Automation slowing R&D | Normal at 7.0+. Set R&D values further ahead to compensate. |
